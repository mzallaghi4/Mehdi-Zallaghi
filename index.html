01 / ABOUT

About me

I am Mehdi Zallaghi, a PhD holder in Applied Mathematics with research interests spanning optimization, financial mathematics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

My interests center on the mathematical foundations and computational tools used to model complex systems, solve challenging decision problems, and extract insight from data. I am particularly interested in work that connects rigorous mathematical analysis with practical applications.

This website brings together my research interests, publications, teaching experience, and technical writing. It is also a point of contact for academic and professional collaborations.
